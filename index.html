Before the first star, before the first planet, ancient powers waited in the darkness. Then Salah awakened—and the universe discovered that its rules were not as unbreakable as it believed.

Alongside Gesheghrs, a robot built to detect the impossible, Salah ventures beyond familiar reality. Strange signals, unknown worlds, powerful guardians, and secrets buried across dimensions begin to reveal a mystery far greater than either of them expected.

But what if the greatest power in the universe is not the only one of its kind?
